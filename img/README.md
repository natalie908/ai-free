# Fotky – Klára (@klara.mezi.radky)

Příspěvky jsou už nahrané: `post-1.png` (knihovna), `post-2.webp` (káva), `post-3.png` (Krebs Cycle of Creativity). Zbývá `avatar.jpg` a `story.jpg`. Prompty na příspěvky níže jsou jen pro případnou výměnu.

Soubory nahraj sem pod přesnými názvy. Dokud fotka chybí, ukazuje profil šedý placeholder s popiskem.

| Soubor | Poměr | Co na ní je |
|---|---|---|
| `avatar.jpg` | 1:1 | profilovka |
| `story.jpg` | 9:16 | jediná story |
| `post-1.jpg` | 4:5 | kniha + káva (nejstarší) |
| `post-2.jpg` | 4:5 | knihovna |
| `post-3.jpg` | 4:5 | čtenářská akce (nejnovější) |

## Postava (vkládat do každého promptu)

> 20-year-old Czech woman, shoulder-length dark blonde hair usually tucked behind one ear, light freckles, no makeup or very little, small silver hoop earrings, thin gold-rimmed round glasses, oversized knit cardigan in muted olive or oatmeal, simple rings on two fingers. Calm, slightly introverted expression, not posing like an influencer.

**Osobitý styl:** zemité tlumené barvy (olivová, béžová, hnědá), brýle, kroužky, vždycky nějaký papír/notes/tužka poblíž. Nic křiklavého.

**Look fotek:** shot on iPhone, natural daylight, slight grain, imperfect framing, no filters, not professional, real-life casual Instagram photo.

## Prompty

**avatar.jpg**
> [postava], casual selfie by a window, soft overcast daylight, half-smile, cardigan collar visible, plain wall behind, shot on iPhone front camera, slight grain, square crop

**post-1.jpg – kniha s kafíčkem**
> top-down photo of an open paperback novel on a wooden café table, a ceramic mug of filter coffee, a pencil and a small notebook with handwritten notes next to it, her hand with rings and cardigan sleeve holding the page, morning light, shot on iPhone, slightly tilted, crumbs on the table, no face

**post-2.jpg – knihovna**
> [postava] standing between tall library shelves, pulling a book out, looking at the spine not at the camera, photo taken by a friend, slightly off-center, warm indoor light, a city library in Brno, shot on iPhone, natural grain

**post-3.jpg – čtenářská akce**
> small reading event in a cozy café-bookshop, rows of mismatched chairs, an author reading at a microphone in the background (out of focus), [postava] seen from behind/side in the audience holding a book and a paper cup, string lights, evening, shot on iPhone from her seat, slightly blurry

**story.jpg – psaní večer**
> laptop on a desk at night with a text document open (text unreadable), a desk lamp, a half-empty mug of tea, sticky notes on the wall, her hands typing, cardigan sleeves, vertical 9:16, shot on iPhone, dim warm light, grain

Na story je v aplikaci navíc text „kapitola 9 ✓" a „23:47 a konečně to dává smysl".
